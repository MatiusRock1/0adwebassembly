## Exact research point at the pause

The pauses are still associated with turns. In the last run, Game::Update with
a turn had a median of 31.81 ms, p95 117.11 ms and maximum 598.48 ms; 597/599 receipts
with a turn exceed 33.4 ms. It is a correlation of receipts and turns, without direct
attribution of physical frames.

The `portable-cache-count-o3` VM passed 21 checks, 9,188 incremental GC yields
and its three exports. `cpu-profile-cache-count-1` recorded 3,885
unimplemented opcodes /32.78 s external: LoadUndefinedResult 839,
GuardMultipleShapes 668 and GuardIndexIsNotDenseElement 340. The counters do not
measure exclusive cost nor every guard failure; they do not justify a broad
backport by themselves.

Those three handlers were investigated in the SpiderMonkey 128.13.0 of the SDK of the
`0ad-wasm-phase4-5:shaders` container. **None was modified nor was another
VM build started before the pause**:

1. LoadUndefinedResult produces UndefinedValue; the current portable handler falls
   back to the fallback. D224672 and the native compiler show the semantics.
2. GuardMultipleShapes receives a ListObject of shapes as a PrivateGCThingValue.
   The native compiler compares the shape with the initialized elements of
   that list. Before porting, check GC creation/tracing and shape changes.
3. GuardIndexIsNotDenseElement passes if the unsigned index is outside the
   initialized length or the element is magic; it fails on a dense element.
   Check negatives, holes, prototypes and accessors, keeping the later guards.

Sources read from the pinned SDK: `jit/CacheIRCompiler.cpp`, `jit/MacroAssembler.cpp`,
`jit/CacheIROps.yaml`, `jit/CacheIRWriter.h`, `vm/List.h`, `js/public/Value.h`.
The downloaded file `wasm/build/phase4-5/cacheir-review/upstream-PortableBaselineInterpret.cpp`
comes from Mozilla's official repository, master without a verified commit: review
only, never a build input. Its hash is in the manifest. The full D224672 patch
